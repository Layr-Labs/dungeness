# Time–space scheduling

## Local scheduling

Häner et al. keep a multiplication or square alive through its sole consumer,
then reverse the producer. They also reuse registers that reach known terminal
constants.

## Phase-level scheduling

Schrottenloher's forward and reverse Euclidean phases have more spare space
than replay, where the transcript and two field values coexist.

## General scheduling

Kornerup et al. formalize ghosted checkpoints and provide exact line-graph
frontiers plus general conversion theorems.

## Weighted interpretation

For arithmetic, annotate:

```text
checkpoint width
recomputation gates
phase-repair gates
dependency order
standard-basis reuse interval
```

The unweighted spooky pebble count remains a theorem about abstract states;
the weighted arithmetic model is a synthesis layer.
