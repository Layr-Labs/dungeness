# Roles of the eight papers

| Paper | Strongest contribution | Main misuse to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Schrottenloher | compact transcript and in-place replay | treating approximation margins as exact guarantees |
| Litinski | signed controlled arithmetic and global corrections | inserting add-subtract as an isolated local replacement |
| Luongo | expected one-bit measured cleanup | generalizing the lemma to arbitrary garbage |
| Gidney | constant-workspace classical-offset addition | applying classical-offset counts to quantum addends |
| Kornerup et al. | formal time–space tradeoffs | treating abstract pebbles as equal-cost arithmetic blocks |
| Luo et al. | exact low-space EEA and field demolition | attributing the complete width to one isolated mechanism |
| Khattar et al. | implicit transition representation | copying polynomial-field counts into integer arithmetic |
| Häner et al. | integrated arithmetic and lifetime scheduling | comparing one component outside its representation shell |

The combined corpus is useful because it places each mechanism next to its
interface and transfer boundary.
