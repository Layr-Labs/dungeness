# Using this corpus

Treat concepts and nodes as navigation aids and the source pages as the
evidence layer.

When retrieving information:

1. identify the required primitive, representation, cleanup rule, or resource
   model;
2. start with `agent-pack.md` or `paper-matrix.md`;
3. read the matching concept and atomic nodes;
4. use the paper section and paper card for context;
5. follow source-page links for exact assumptions;
6. keep quantities in the cost model used by their source;
7. distinguish direct evidence from cross-paper synthesis.

Do not infer that similarly named primitives are interchangeable. Check:

- input and output placement;
- field or ring representation;
- classical versus quantum operands;
- control semantics;
- retained history or garbage;
- measurement and phase-repair obligations;
- forward and inverse behavior;
- width, gate-count, and depth conventions.

The corpus supplies published mechanisms and source evidence only. Any
application, composition, implementation, or evaluation must be derived
outside this knowledge base.
