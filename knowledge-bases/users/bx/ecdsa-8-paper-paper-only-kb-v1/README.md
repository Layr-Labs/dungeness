# Eight-paper quantum arithmetic knowledge base

This edition is restricted to eight named papers. It contains:

1. unchanged copies of the eight PDFs;
2. page-by-page searchable text extracted from those PDFs;
3. eight compact paper cards whose factual claims cite source pages;
4. 29 paper sections;
5. 80 atomic mechanism nodes;
6. 20 cross-paper concept pages;
7. a graph, paper matrix, experiment map, and compact agent pack.

It intentionally contains no solution artifacts, implementation source,
result history, external notes, auxiliary software, or application-specific
adaptation advice.

## Evidence labels

- **[EVIDENCE]** is a paraphrase of a cited passage in one of the eight PDFs.
- **[SYNTHESIS]** combines cited facts from two or more of the eight PDFs
  without asserting an experimental result.

If a concise card is ambiguous, follow its page link and inspect the extracted
page or the unchanged PDF.
