# Litinski transfer boundaries

The controlled add-subtract saving is global: signed branch effects are
removed by corrections derived for the complete multiplication identity.

The principal multiplication interfaces use a fresh output, and the
prime-modulus construction uses Montgomery representation and window garbage.
Copying and reversing may be required for a clean reusable result.
