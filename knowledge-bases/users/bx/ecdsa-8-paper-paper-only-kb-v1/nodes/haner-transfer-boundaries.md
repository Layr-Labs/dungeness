# Häner transfer boundaries

Montgomery multiplication changes the representation of every value that
remains inside the arithmetic shell. Signed-window lookup belongs to the
paper's complete scalar-multiplication wrapper.

The most representation-independent mechanisms are producer-consumer fusion,
workspace reuse after terminal constants appear, and correction work scheduled
inside reverse traversal.
