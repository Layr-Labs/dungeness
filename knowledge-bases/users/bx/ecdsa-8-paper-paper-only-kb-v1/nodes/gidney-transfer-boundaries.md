# Gidney transfer boundaries

The adder requires a classically specified offset. Its dirty-workspace variant
also requires borrowed qubits that are available throughout the operation and
restored exactly.

Prime-field use additionally needs a comparison and correction shell. The
carry measurements and phase-repair circuit are part of the primitive.
