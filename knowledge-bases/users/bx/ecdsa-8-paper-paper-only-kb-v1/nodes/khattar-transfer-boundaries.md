# Khattar transfer boundaries

The Dialog and synchronized EEA are defined over polynomial fields. An integer
prime-field version needs a new transition alphabet, sign and quotient
encoding, bit-length invariant, and resource analysis.

Transfer the implicit-linear-transformation principle, not the polynomial
operation counts.
