# Ghosting semantics

A coherent checkpoint may be Hadamard-measured and released. Its measurement outcome is stored as a classical ghost.

To remove the ghost later:

```text
recompute the checkpoint predicate
apply the outcome-controlled phase correction
uncompute the reconstruction
```

Ghosting lowers qubit storage but not total classical-plus-quantum information.
