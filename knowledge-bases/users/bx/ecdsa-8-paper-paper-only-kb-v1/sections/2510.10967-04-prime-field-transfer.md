# Khattar et al. — prime-field transfer

The published costs use polynomial EEA over `GF(2^b)`. Secp256k1 uses integers modulo a 256-bit prime.

Transfer requires new answers for:

- the small transition alphabet;
- quotient/sign storage;
- integer comparison and halving;
- modular coefficient growth;
- direct division output placement;
- whether each transition sign can be recovered from retained state.
