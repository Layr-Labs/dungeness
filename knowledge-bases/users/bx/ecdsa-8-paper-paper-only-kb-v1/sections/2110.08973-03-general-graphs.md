# Spooky Pebbling — general graphs and trees

An irreversible pebbling using `s` pebbles and `T` steps can be converted into
a spooky pebbling using at most `s+1` pebbles and `O(nT)` steps.

For arbitrary directed acyclic graphs, approximating the minimum spooky pebble
number is PSPACE-hard. For a complete binary tree with `n=2^h-1`, the paper
gives an `h+1`-pebble strategy using `O(n log n)` steps.
