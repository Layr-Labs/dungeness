# Häner et al. — arithmetic components

The paper provides interchangeable width-, T-count-, and depth-oriented arithmetic:

- integer and controlled addition;
- constant addition;
- Montgomery multiplication;
- integrated multiply-then-add;
- squaring consumed directly by subtraction.
