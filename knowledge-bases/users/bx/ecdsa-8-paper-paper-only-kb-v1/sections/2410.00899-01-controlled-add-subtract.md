# Litinski — controlled add-subtract

## Mechanism

**[PAPER]** An ordinary ripple adder can implement:

```text
control=1 -> add a
control=0 -> subtract a
```

by complementing the addend through multi-target Clifford controls before and after the adder. With no carry-out, this costs `n-1` Toffolis instead of `2n-1` for a controlled add-or-do-nothing circuit.

The cheaper local branch changes the global algebra. The paper derives correction terms that turn the signed sum into multiplication.
