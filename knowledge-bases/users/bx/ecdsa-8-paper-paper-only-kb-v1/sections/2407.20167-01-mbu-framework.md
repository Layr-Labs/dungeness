# Luongo et al. — MBU framework

## Lemma

For a one-bit Boolean garbage function, X-basis measurement removes the value. One outcome introduces no phase; the other creates a phase by `g(x)` and invokes the self-adjoint garbage oracle for repair.

The expensive repair branch occurs with probability one half, so costs are expected values.

## Required semantics

The exact measurement result, phase repair, and conditional branch are part of
the construction. A static gate-list reversal does not by itself specify the
inverse of a measured circuit.
