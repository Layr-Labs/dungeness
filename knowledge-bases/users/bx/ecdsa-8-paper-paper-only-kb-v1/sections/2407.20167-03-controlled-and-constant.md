# Luongo et al. — controlled and constant arithmetic

The paper applies its one-bit measurement-based cleanup rule to controlled,
constant, and controlled-constant modular adders.

A central structural observation is that an outer control need not be attached
to every internal operation. Some comparisons and corrections are determined
by the arithmetic state once the controlled boundary operation has occurred.
