# arXiv:2407.20167v1 — Measurement-based uncomputation of quantum circuits for modular arithmetic

## Citation

Alessandro Luongo, Antonio Michele Miti, Varun Narasimhachar, and Adithya Sireesh, “Measurement-based uncomputation of quantum circuits for modular arithmetic,” arXiv:2407.20167v1 (2024).

## Scope in this knowledge base

Measure a one-bit predicate, accept the free branch, and invoke a self-adjoint repair computation only on the phase-error branch.

**Headline result:** Expected costs: \(3.5n\) for Gidney modular addition and \(4.5n+0.5\) for its controlled form.

**Primary transfer warning:** The repair must implement the one-bit self-adjoint XOR-oracle contract; expected cost assumes equiprobable measurement branches.
