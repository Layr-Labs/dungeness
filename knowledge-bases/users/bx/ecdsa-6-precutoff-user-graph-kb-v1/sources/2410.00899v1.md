# arXiv:2410.00899v1 — Quantum schoolbook multiplication with fewer Toffoli gates

## Citation

Daniel Litinski, “Quantum schoolbook multiplication with fewer Toffoli gates,” arXiv:2410.00899v1 (2024).

## Scope in this knowledge base

Replace shifted controlled additions by controlled add-subtract operations and repair the aggregate affine offset globally.

**Headline result:** Non-modular multiplier: \(n^2+4n+3\) Toffolis.

**Primary transfer warning:** The prime-modulus construction is out-of-place, uses Montgomery representation, and produces garbage.
