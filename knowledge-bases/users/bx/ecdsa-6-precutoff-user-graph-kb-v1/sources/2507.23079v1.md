# arXiv:2507.23079v1 — A Classical-Quantum Adder with Constant Workspace and Linear Gates

## Citation

Craig Gidney, “A Classical-Quantum Adder with Constant Workspace and Linear Gates,” arXiv:2507.23079v1 (2025).

## Scope in this knowledge base

Vent carry garbage into deferred phase corrections and trade clean workspace for dirty borrowing.

**Headline result:** \(4n\pm O(1)\) with three clean ancillas, or \(3n\pm O(1)\) with two clean and \(n-2\) dirty ancillas.

**Primary transfer warning:** The construction is a classical-offset adder; the measurement outcomes create phase-correction obligations.
