# IACR ePrint 2020/077 — Improved quantum circuits for elliptic curve discrete logarithms

## Citation

Thomas Häner, Samuel Jaques, Michael Naehrig, Martin Roetteler, and Mathias Soeken, “Improved quantum circuits for elliptic curve discrete logarithms,” IACR ePrint 2020/077 (2020).

## Scope in this knowledge base

Normalize four Kaliski branches into one swapped round body, run a fixed schedule, and fuse correction and cleanup.

**Headline result:** Exactly \(2n\) scheduled inversion rounds; \(2n-k\) modular doublings repair the pseudo-inverse during reverse cleanup.

**Primary transfer warning:** The inversion and multiplication stack uses Montgomery-form arithmetic and tightly coupled register lifetimes.
