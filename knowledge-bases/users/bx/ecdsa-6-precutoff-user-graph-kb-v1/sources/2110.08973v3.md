# arXiv:2110.08973v3 — Tight Bounds on the Spooky Pebble Game: Recycling Qubits with Measurements

## Citation

Niels Kornerup, Jonathan Sadun, and David Soloveichik, “Tight Bounds on the Spooky Pebble Game: Recycling Qubits with Measurements,” arXiv:2110.08973v3.

## Scope in this knowledge base

Recycle qubits by replacing a computed value with a phase ghost indexed by retained classical measurement data.

**Headline result:** A line satisfying \(n\leq\binom{m+s-2}{s-2}+1\) admits an \(s\)-pebble, \(O(mn)\)-step strategy.

**Primary transfer warning:** Quantum space is recycled, but the classical measurement record remains live until unghosting.
