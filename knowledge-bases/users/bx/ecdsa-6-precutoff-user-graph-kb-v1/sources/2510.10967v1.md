## Citation

Tanuj Khattar et al., “Verifiable Quantum Advantage via Optimized DQI Circuits,” arXiv:2510.10967v1 (2025).

## Scope in this knowledge base

Represent the EEA as an executable transition trace (“Dialog”) and replay it on a different vector to divide directly.

**Headline result:** Direct division applies the EEA transition product to \([0,A]^T\), avoiding a materialized \(B^{-1}\).

**Primary transfer warning:** The quantitative analysis is specialized to polynomial arithmetic over \(\mathrm{GF}(2^b)\), not large-prime integer arithmetic.
