# Swap-normalized Kaliski round

The binary Kaliski inverse is reformulated so cases share a common round surrounded by controlled swaps and simple controls.

This reduces duplicated conditional arithmetic and gives a fixed schedule of up to `2n` rounds.

**[BENCHMARK]** A later local Kaliski-style coefficient variant grew enough to become the peak owner, so the conceptual normalization does not by itself guarantee a current win.
