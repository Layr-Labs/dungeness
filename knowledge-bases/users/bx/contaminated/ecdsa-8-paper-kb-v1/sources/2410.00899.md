# Source: arXiv:2410.00899

## One-paragraph synthesis

**[PAPER]** Litinski replaces each controlled addition in schoolbook multiplication with a controlled add-subtract. Because the two branches are addition and subtraction instead of addition and identity, the control can be implemented through Clifford bit complements around an ordinary ripple adder. This reduces the controlled primitive from `2n-1` Toffolis to `n-1` when no carry-out is retained. Global algebraic correction terms convert the resulting signed accumulation into the desired product.

## Main counts

| Operation | Proposed Toffoli count | Compared construction |
|---|---:|---:|
| controlled add-subtract, no carry-out | `n-1` | controlled addition `2n-1` |
| `n × n -> 2n` schoolbook product | `n^2 + 4n + 3` | `2n^2 + n` |
| product modulo `2^n` | `0.5n^2 + 1.5n` | `n^2` |
| prime-modulus Montgomery product | `n^2 + 6n + (n/w)(2^w + 3·2^(w/2) + 3n - 3)` | roughly double the leading schoolbook term |

The suggested window is approximately:

```text
w ~= log2(n / log2(n)) + 2
```

**[PAPER]** The paper reports up to roughly 30% fewer Toffolis in cited 256-bit ECDLP circuit estimates when the new multipliers replace standard schoolbook multipliers.

## Transfer boundary

The paper's core multiplication interface is:

```text
|x>|y>|0> -> |x>|y>|xy>
```

The current replay shell needs in-place multiplication/division behavior. A fresh accumulator is therefore a peak-width liability, and cleaning or transforming it into an in-place result may require the same inversion machinery being optimized.

## Source limitations

- The prime-modulus construction assumes Montgomery representation and a windowed lookup/reduction shell.
- The headline counts are logical Toffoli counts, not average executed benchmark counts.
- The paper does not solve the in-place output-placement problem.
- The ECDLP percentage is an integrated estimate, not an `ecdsa.fail` result.
