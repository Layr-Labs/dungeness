# Source: arXiv:2507.23079

## One-paragraph synthesis

**[PAPER]** Gidney gives the first classical-quantum constant-offset adders with both linear Toffoli count and constant clean workspace. Carry qubits are streamed and measured in the X basis as soon as they become redundant, turning them into deferred phase tasks. A later carry-xor computation repairs those phases. A target register can be split into halves so each half serves as dirty workspace while the other is updated.

## Main results

| Variant | Clean ancillas | Dirty ancillas | Toffolis |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| constant clean-workspace adder | 3 | 0 | `4n ± O(1)` |
| dirty-workspace adder | 2 | `n-2` | `3n ± O(1)` |

**[PAPER]** Both constructions can be controlled without additional Toffolis or workspace. Their depth is linear.

## Three mechanisms

1. **Venting carries:** X-basis measurement removes a carry value but creates a possible phase by that carry.
2. **Carry-xor repair:** interleave phase flips with a circuit that xors the carry pattern into a secondary register.
3. **Borrowing back and forth:** split the target; update one half while borrowing the other as dirty workspace, then swap roles.

## Transfer boundary

The offset must be classical. The strongest benchmark candidates are modulus corrections, fixed pseudo-Mersenne folds, conditional constant offsets, and any current primitive that allocates a long clean carry ladder at the peak.

## Source limitations

- The dynamic phase-repair semantics must be reproduced exactly.
- The current replay path may already use an `n-1`-Toffoli quantum-quantum adder floor; this paper does not beat that operand model.
- Dirty borrowing helps only when the borrowed half remains available and is exactly restored.
- Linear depth can matter if depth or measurement latency enters a future physical metric, even though it is not the present score.
