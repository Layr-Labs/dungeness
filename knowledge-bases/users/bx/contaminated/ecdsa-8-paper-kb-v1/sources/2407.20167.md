# Source: arXiv:2407.20167

## One-paragraph synthesis

**[PAPER]** The paper formalizes measurement-based uncomputation for a single Boolean garbage qubit and applies it systematically to modular addition, controlled modular addition, constant modular addition, and comparison. Measuring the garbage qubit in the X basis deletes its value but may leave a phase. One measurement outcome needs no expensive recomputation; the other invokes a self-adjoint garbage oracle to repair the phase, producing a one-half expected invocation.

## MBU lemma

For:

```text
Ug |x>|b> = |x>|b xor g(x)>
```

the state:

```text
sum_x alpha_x |x>|g(x)>
```

can be returned to:

```text
sum_x alpha_x |x>|0>
```

using an X-basis measurement and a branch-dependent phase repair. The costly `Ug` branch occurs with probability one half.

## Representative modular-adder counts

| Architecture | Without MBU | With MBU, expected |
|---|---:|---:|
| CDKPM modular add | `8n` | `7n` |
| Gidney modular add | `4n` | `3.5n` |
| mixed CDKPM+Gidney | `6n` | `5.5n` |
| CDKPM controlled modular add | about `9n+1` | `8n+0.5` |
| Gidney controlled modular add | `5n+1` | about `4.5n+0.5` |

The paper also derives constant and controlled-constant modular adders and a two-sided comparator suitable for MBU.

## Transfer boundary

**[INFERENCE]** The paper is most relevant to one-bit predicates in replay corrections: comparison flags, overflow/fold flags, carry boundaries, or a complete field register only when its phase predicate can be reconstructed.

## Source limitations

- Costs are expectations over measurement outcomes.
- The benchmark must support measurement, classical outcomes, reset, and conditional phase correction with the assumed cost.
- Mechanical gate-list reversal is not a valid inverse of a dynamic MBU circuit.
- The paper does not establish that its modular-adder wrapper matches the current ping-pong representation.
