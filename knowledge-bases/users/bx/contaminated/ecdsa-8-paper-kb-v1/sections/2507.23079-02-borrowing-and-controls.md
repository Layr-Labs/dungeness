# Gidney — borrowing and controls

Split the target into two halves:

1. update the lower half while borrowing the upper half as dirty workspace;
2. propagate the cross-half carry;
3. update the upper half while borrowing the completed lower half;
4. repair all vented phases.

Results:

```text
3 clean, no dirty: 4n ± O(1) Toffoli
2 clean, n-2 dirty: 3n ± O(1) Toffoli
control: no extra Toffoli or workspace
```
