# Luongo et al. — benchmark transfer

## Candidate targets

**[INFERENCE]**

- truncated comparator result;
- modular-fold overflow flag;
- chunk-boundary carry;
- condition bit whose predicate can be cheaply recomputed.

## Rejection rule

Do not use MBU when:

- the predicate oracle is not self-adjoint or cheap;
- the measurement result cannot survive to the inverse;
- the phase correction is invisible to basis-only tests;
- the conditional branch is charged differently from the paper's expectation.
