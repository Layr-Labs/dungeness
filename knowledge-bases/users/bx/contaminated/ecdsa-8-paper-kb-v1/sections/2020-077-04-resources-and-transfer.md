# Häner et al. — resources and transfer

For P-256 ECDLP, the low-width design reports 2124 logical qubits, down from 2338, with major T-count and T-depth reductions over RNSL.

**[INFERENCE]** Use this paper as:

- an end-to-end control;
- a source of lifetime fusion patterns;
- a warning that primitive costs depend on the surrounding shell.

Do not treat its complete circuit as the expected benchmark winner.
