# Spooky Pebbling — benchmark transfer

The walk is line-like, but its checkpoint is a wide `(u,v)` pair and its edge costs shrink over time. The replay also consumes signs in a direction-dependent order.

**[BENCHMARK]** Generic checkpointing has already added about 30% Toffoli without lowering the realized peak. The productive transfer is therefore:

```text
derive the exact dependency DAG
price nonuniform checkpoints
interleave replay with history production
ghost only predicates with cheap phase oracles
```
