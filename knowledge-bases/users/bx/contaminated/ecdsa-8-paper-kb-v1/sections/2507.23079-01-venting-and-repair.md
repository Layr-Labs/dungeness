# Gidney — venting and repair

Carries are Z-basis functions of the final sum and classical offset. X-basis measurement can therefore free each carry while leaving a possible phase task. The later carry-xor circuit reproduces the carry pattern and converts controlled target flips plus interleaved Z gates into the required phase correction.
