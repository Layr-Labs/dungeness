# Luongo et al. — modular adders

The paper builds modular arithmetic from plain adders, subtractors, and comparators, then applies MBU to the final one-bit predicate.

Representative expected Toffoli reductions:

```text
CDKPM modular add:        8n -> 7n
Gidney modular add:       4n -> 3.5n
mixed modular add:        6n -> 5.5n
CDKPM controlled mod add: ~9n+1 -> 8n+0.5
```
