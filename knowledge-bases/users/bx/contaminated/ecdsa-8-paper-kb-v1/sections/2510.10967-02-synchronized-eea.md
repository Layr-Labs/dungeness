# Khattar et al. — synchronized EEA

Quotient coefficients are stored inside space freed by the shrinking polynomial state. Localized swaps merge quotient extraction and state update into one fixed schedule.

Reported leading resources:

```text
2nb + O(log^2 n) qubits
6n worst-case synchronized iterations
6n^2 field multiplications
```
