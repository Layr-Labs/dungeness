# Paper role map

Use this page for orientation, then retrieve by decision dossier.

| Source | Strongest role | It does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Schrottenloher | low-T/Q prime-field in-place history/replay spine | optimal recurrence, exact benchmark success, or optimal schedule |
| Litinski | add-versus-subtract control algebra and lower-Toffoli multiplication | drop-in in-place replay or free global corrections |
| Luongo | formal one-bit measurement-based uncomputation | free deletion of arbitrary wide garbage |
| Gidney | constant-offset addition with constant clean workspace and phase-repaired carries | improvement for quantum-quantum addends |
| Spooky Pebbling | abstract time/qubit frontier with measured ghosts | superiority of generic checkpointing in a weighted arithmetic circuit |
| Luo | explicit low-space EEA, active windows, and full-register recycling | a low score product when ported literally |
| Khattar | explicit/implicit EEA state and Dialog-based direct division | prime-field transition rules or secp256k1 counts |
| Häner | mature whole-stack dependency scheduling and workspace reuse | a 2026 frontier arithmetic implementation |

## Retrieval triggers

### Schrottenloher

Retrieve when the question concerns:

```text
binary-Euclid history
linear replay
secp256k1 folds
in-place multiplication/division
approximate exactness regimes
```

### Litinski

Retrieve when a hot branch naturally selects add versus subtract, or when a
fresh output accumulator can be consumed outside the peak.

### Luongo

Retrieve when one Boolean cleanup oracle is expensive and exactly
reconstructible. Use it to define phase-correct expected-cost semantics.

### Gidney

Retrieve when the addend is a classical constant, clean carry storage owns the
peak, or a target half can safely act as restored dirty workspace.

### Spooky Pebbling

Retrieve when a wide intermediate is retained across a long dependency chain
and the choice is coherent storage versus recomputation plus phase repair.

### Luo

Retrieve when width is dominated by field-register liveness, moving active
subranges, or an obsolete value that is exactly recomputable.

### Khattar

Retrieve when the question is whether quotient or transition data can replace
explicit coefficients, or whether division can act through an implicit
transform.

### Häner

Retrieve when a primitive boundary creates a copied result, two overlapping
workspaces, or an uncomputation that could be delayed until after the final
consumer.

## Cross-paper reading rule

Read a second paper only to answer a conflict raised by the first:

```text
Schrottenloher tape too wide
  -> Luo, Khattar, or Spooky

replay arithmetic too expensive
  -> Litinski, Gidney, or Häner

cleanup dominates
  -> Luongo, Gidney, Luo, or Spooky

affine shell retains too many field values
  -> Häner, Luo, or Khattar
```

This keeps retrieval attached to a measured problem rather than to paper order.
